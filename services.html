Frequently Asked Questions

What file formats are required to begin?

I primarily work with .3dm (Rhino), .rvt (Revit), .dwg (AutoCAD), and .skp (SketchUp) files. High-resolution PDFs of architectural plans and elevations are also helpful for cross-referencing fine details.

Do you create both physical and digital models?

My specialisation is physical scale models — tangible, hand-crafted objects. However, all models begin with digital preparation in Rhino 3D and AutoCAD before physical fabrication begins. I do not produce standalone digital renders, but can recommend trusted visualisation partners.

What is the typical lead time for each model type?

Concept models: 1–2 weeks. Residential presentation models: 2–4 weeks. Commercial and urban planning models: 4–8 weeks. Premium exhibition models: 8–12 weeks. All timelines are confirmed in writing at the quoting stage.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes — I have delivered models to clients in 18 countries across Europe, the Middle East, Asia, and North America. Models are crated in custom foam-lined timber boxes and shipped via specialist art courier services with full transit insurance.

Can I visit the studio during the build?

Studio visits are welcome for Standard and Premium tier projects. For Concept tier projects, weekly photo updates are provided instead. All clients receive regular photographic progress reports throughout the build.
