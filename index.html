From Blueprint to Delivery

A transparent 5-step process managed personally by me from start to finish.

01

Blueprint & File Review

I review your CAD/BIM files (.dwg, .rvt, .3dm), architectural drawings, and project brief. We discuss scale, materials, level of detail, and key presentation goals.

02

Digital Fabrication Prep

Your files are optimised in Rhino 3D and exported as vector paths for laser cutting. 3D printed components are sliced and validated in Chitubox/PrusaSlicer for accuracy.

03

Material Selection & Procurement

I select materials that best represent your design — sustainably sourced timbers, architectural-grade acrylics, UV-resistant resins, etched brass, and powder-coated steel for structural elements.

04

Artisanal Assembly & Finishing

Components are assembled under magnification, joined with precision adhesives, hand-sanded, painted to specified RAL codes, and finished with model-grade varnishes. Progress photos are shared regularly.

05

Photography, Crating & Delivery

Final quality inspection, professional studio photography for your records, then custom crating in foam-lined timber cases. White-glove courier delivery worldwide with full insurance.
